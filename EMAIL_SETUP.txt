EMAIL SETUP
===========

Faculty 48-hour lock email is implemented.

Before testing real Gmail delivery:
1. Open backend/shared/email_config.php
2. Set MAIL_USERNAME to the Gmail account you will use as sender.
3. Set MAIL_APP_PASSWORD to that Gmail account's 16-character Google App Password.
4. In users table, make sure the assigned advisor/faculty has a valid email in users.email.
5. Save the file and test again.

Do not use your normal Gmail password. Do not share the App Password or commit it to GitHub.

The faculty lock flow:
Student applies -> advisor_status=4 Pending -> after 48 hours -> advisor_status=2 Locked -> email to assigned faculty only.
Locked requests are not forwarded to HOD.
